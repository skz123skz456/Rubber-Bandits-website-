Who we are

Building Robots. Building People. Building Community.

We are FTC Team 17288 — the Rubber Bandits, a student-led FIRST Tech Challenge team from Orlando, Florida. Since our rookie season in 2019, we’ve brought together students interested in engineering, programming, design, business, and STEM outreach.

But we’re more than just a robot

Our team is built around the idea that robotics should be an opportunity to learn, create, collaborate, and give back.

Every member contributes

From designing and programming our competition robot to managing sponsors and organizing community events, every member contributes to making the team successful.

We compete, we build, we fail, we rebuild, and we keep learning.

And, of course, we do it all with a little bit of LARP.

Outreach impact

More than a robot

For us, outreach isn’t something we do because it’s required. It’s one of the biggest parts of what makes the Rubber Bandits who we are.

Over the years, our team has participated in outreach events throughout Central Florida and beyond, bringing robotics directly to students, families, educators, and professionals.

Where we’ve made an impact

Our team has participated in events and partnerships including:

  • I/ITSEC — introducing attendees to FIRST and hands-on robotics
  • Women in Aviation International
  • Space Coast Foundation
  • Maker Faire Orlando
  • School and community STEM events
  • Robotics demonstrations and showcases
  • Youth STEM activities
  • Community education events
  • Featured on Spectrum News 13 while co-hosting our summer camp
  • And many more throughout Central Florida

At these events, people don’t just watch our robot. They get to drive it, interact with the team, ask questions, learn how it works, and see what student-led engineering looks like behind the scenes.

We’ve also used hands-on activities, including LEGO/FLL-style robotics activities, to give younger students an opportunity to build and experiment themselves.

Our goal

Every outreach event gives us another opportunity to show someone that STEM isn’t just a class at school. It’s something you can build, explore, compete in, and make a career out of.

Whether we’re talking to hundreds of people at a large conference or helping one student figure out how a motor works, we want people to leave our events thinking:

“I could do this.”

02 · Team history

From 2019 to today

The Rubber Bandits began competing in the FIRST Tech Challenge in 2019. Since then, Team 17288 has competed across multiple FTC seasons, growing from a rookie team into an established Orlando robotics program.

According to the official FIRST FTC Event Web, the Rubber Bandits have competed in 40 official events across seven seasons, beginning with the 2019 SKYSTONE season and continuing through DECODE in 2025–26.

Our story isn’t finished

Seven seasons in, the Rubber Bandits are still building. Every season brings a new game, a new robot, new challenges, and a new group of students ready to take them on.

2019 → 2026 → and we’re just getting started.
